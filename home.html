About this calculator

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is calculated from height and weight and can be one piece of a broader health assessment. It cannot describe your whole health, fitness, or wellbeing.

Everyday wellness reminders

Stay hydrated

Keep water nearby and drink regularly throughout your day.

🥗

Eat a variety

Enjoy a variety of foods that help you feel nourished and satisfied.

🌿

Move in ways you enjoy

Choose activities that feel good to you, at a pace that suits you.

🌙

Make room for rest

A regular sleep routine can support your mood, focus, and energy.
